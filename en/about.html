My story

GrandpaGames is more than just a website for me. It's a place where I bring together my ideas, the joy of creating, and the desire to create something of my own.

I got into programming gradually, but the real turning point came when I wanted to create something personal. Something that wouldn't just be a "project" but would have its own story.

My first game was a memory game for my girlfriend, created from our shared photos as a gift made with love. Later, a version called Forest was created, this time with my own pictures of nature.

Master Catcher was created in a similar way. A simple game, but with one idea – to make another game for my girlfriend. A game that can entertain you for a while and bring a smile to your face. And yes… the characters in the game are really us 😊

GrandpaGames is still at the beginning. But I have many ideas, and I want to gradually turn them into games with their own atmosphere and story.

I am constantly trying to learn, improve, and turn my ideas into real projects.

Thank you for your feedback and support.
