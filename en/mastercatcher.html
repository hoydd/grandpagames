About the game

Master Catcher is my own game where the player catches falling objects, earns points and tries to last as long as possible. I wanted to create a simple but fun game that anyone can launch quickly and start playing right away.

Game features

  • fast and simple gameplay
  • score collecting
  • improving your high score
  • original visual style
